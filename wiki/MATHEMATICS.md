# Mathematics 1.0 and Mathematics 2.0

Working terminology for creating mathematical knowledge with humans and AI.

**Mathematics 1.0:** human-led formulation, reasoning and verification, with existing computational tools.

**Mathematics 2.0:** the proposed workflow in which agents help formulate definitions, conjectures, proofs and counterexamples, while results receive explicit verification.

## A result must include

The statement, definitions, assumptions, proof or computation, verification method, dependencies and reproduction instructions.

Formal proofs should identify the proof assistant and version. Numerical results should identify data, code, environment and precision. Candidate output and independently checked results are separate states.

[Lean](https://lean-lang.org/) is one available open-source proof assistant; a checked proof is relative to its formal statement and assumptions.
