# Understanding each other

Coordinate around what people state and agree to.

For a neighbor, start with a shared situation: a noise problem, a common task or an offer of help. State the observable issue and one proposed next step. Give the other person room to describe needs, boundaries and corrections.

Use a human model only with its owner's agreement. Keep what someone said separate from your interpretation. Record disagreements without turning them into diagnoses or claims about hidden motives.

Agreement on the next action does not require agreement on everything. A refusal or no response does not establish a motive.
