# Psychology

Build evidence-based accounts of behavior, needs, learning, cooperation and distress.

The initial research map covers attention and effort, learning and feedback, emotion and distress, social interaction, motivation, and the influence of institutions and environment.

Separate a reported experience from an interpretation, a research finding and a proposed intervention. Record the population, method and limitations of a study. A metaphor such as the monkey brain is a design cue, not a diagnosis.

For a person facing a difficulty, begin with [getting help](GETTING_HELP.md). For interactions with others, use [understanding each other](UNDERSTANDING_EACH_OTHER.md).

WHO describes mental health as part of well-being and emphasizes individual, community and structural influences. [Source](https://www.who.int/health-topics/mental-health), checked 12 October 2026.
