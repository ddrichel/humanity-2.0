# Getting help

Start with one concrete situation.

Write what is happening, what outcome you need, and what blocks it. Name only constraints that affect the next action.

Ask a trusted human or an agent to identify one feasible step and explain what it changes. Let the helper gather information and prepare reversible work within your authorization. Keep the result visible so the next helper can continue without making you repeat everything.

After the action, update the situation. If the first route fails, record the blocker and try the next relevant route.

## When the difficulty concerns health

Use a qualified professional for assessment and treatment. The local help directory should use official sources, with country, language and last-checked date.

Germany: medical practices are the normal point of contact. For urgent non-life-threatening medical help when practices are closed, 116117 provides the on-call service in German. For a life-threatening emergency or risk of permanent injury, call 112. [Official service and practitioner search](https://www.116117.de/de/englisch.php), checked 12 October 2026.

Contribute verified routes for other countries. No personal health details are required in a public issue.
