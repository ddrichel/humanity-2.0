# Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Goal | Current state | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01: Human welfare | Make a voluntary welfare check and useful help accessible to any human. | Protocol and help pages published; outreach and software not implemented. | People can find a relevant help route, choose a next action, decline and correct the process. |
| 02: Human model | Let a person inspect and revise a contextual model of themselves. | Prototype schema and synthetic example. | Owner review and correction demonstrated; publication stays optional. |
| 03: Mathematical knowledge | Create candidate mathematics with independent verification. | Research protocol and benchmark questions. | Reproducible checked result with explicit assumptions. |
| 04: Forecasts | Compare dated predictions with outcomes. | Initial Tesla hardware records. | Dated evidence updates the outcome without rewriting the original forecast. |

Human welfare is the first checkpoint, not a claim that humanity has already been checked. Coverage, unanswered requests and actual help outcomes remain separate.
