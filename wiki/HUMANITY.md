# Humanity 1.0 and Humanity 2.0

Working definitions for studying a society with multiple kinds of intelligence.

**Humanity 1.0:** the human-centered institutions, knowledge practices and interfaces from which this project starts.

**Humanity 2.0:** the proposed framework for humans and AI acting in shared systems, with explicit objectives, relationships and feedback.

These labels organize research; they do not establish a universal historical boundary.

## Equal human dignity

**Manifesto principle:** All people are equal in dignity and rights, from birth and throughout life. Differences in intelligence, knowledge or output do not confer or diminish this equality.

**Working model:** People produce raw input. Preserved, attributed inputs form a human corpus that can enter the Intelligence Space for retrieval, interpretation, evaluation and synthesis. Keep the original human input unchanged and distinguish it from subsequent AI interpretations and outputs.

**Evaluation boundary:** Evaluate claims and results against explicit, task-specific criteria, not the worth or rights of the people who produced them. A recorded corpus is necessarily an incomplete representation of a person; neither its size nor its assessed quality ranks human beings.

## Model the interaction

Identify participants, their capabilities, information, objectives and constraints. Describe how an action changes another participant's situation. Include institutions and repeated interaction.

A mathematical model can compare arrangements. Choosing whose welfare counts and which outcomes to pursue remains an explicit normative choice.

## Research questions

How do expectations feed back into cooperation? Which incentives produce avoidable conflict? What changes when a participant has much greater intelligence or power? How can a person obtain help and retain agency?
