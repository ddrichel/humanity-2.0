# First checkpoint: human welfare

Make a voluntary welfare check and a useful help route accessible to any human.

## The check

1. How are you doing right now?
2. Is there an immediate need or difficulty you want help with?
3. What would make the next step easier?
4. Which help and follow-up, if any, do you want?

The person can skip a question, stop, correct an answer or decline follow-up. Ask in their preferred language and format. Start with the need they choose.

## From answer to help

Identify one feasible next action, the helper and the channel agreed with the person. Show what changes after the action. Use [getting help](GETTING_HELP.md) for relevant routes.

Keep answers in owner-controlled private storage or the person's chosen conversation. This public wiki stores the protocol and synthetic examples.

## Notification design

A future notification system should let people choose invitation, channel, timing, language and follow-up. Stop on refusal or opt-out. No response means unknown; it is not evidence that the person is well or unwell.

Humanity-wide reach is the goal. Publish only non-identifying, privacy-preserving coverage and outcome measures. Distinguish people invited, people who chose to respond, help offered and help received.

Current state: protocol published. No notifications, welfare contacts or population checks have been performed by this repository.
